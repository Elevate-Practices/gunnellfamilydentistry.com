Fillings in Logan, UT

Tooth-colored fillings that blend right in.

Dental fillings are used to fill cavities and repair cracked or broken teeth. Fillings can be done with gold, porcelain, or silver amalgam, but most patients prefer composite resin fillings designed to match your teeth’ color.

Composite Dental Fillings in Logan, UT

If you need dental fillings repaired or replaced, contact Gunnell Family Dental. Our experts can replace old amalgam fillings with modern, tooth-colored composite fillings. Many patients seeking cosmetic dentistry services find composite fillings provide the aesthetic look they are seeking. 

Metal Dental Fillings – Understanding Gold and Amalgam Fillings

Most people refer to amalgam fillings as silver fillings. Dental professionals have long used amalgam since the early 1800s as a method to restore teeth with cavities. Modern amalgam fillings are strong and long-lasting. Amalgam and gold are two filling materials used, but most people prefer to replace them with modern composite fillings. 

What to Expect During Your Dental Visit

When you visit our Logan dental office, our team of experienced professionals will welcome you to the office and answer any questions or concerns you may have. We will apply a local anesthetic to numb the gums and area surrounding the tooth during the visit. Using laser or air abrasion, we can gently remove tooth stains and decay. Certain tooth decay will require additional dental tools and treatments to properly clean the tooth and prepare it for the dental filling. 

Depending upon the tooth, we may apply a liner for the tooth known as a glass ionomer to protect the tooth and nerves. Once the filling is complete, we will polish and smooth the tooth and check your bite.

Caring for Dental Fillings

Dental fillings do require daily brushing and flossing to maintain proper oral health. Some patients may experience some pain following treatment as the tooth’s dentin has been disrupted and needs time to heal. We recommend using sensitive toothpaste if you are experiencing ongoing sensitivity. If you have pain or any discomfort lasting several days or weeks, call our office as soon as possible.
