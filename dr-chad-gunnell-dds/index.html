Dr. Chad Gunnell

DDS

Dr. Chad Gunnell, Graduated from Utah State University with a bachelor’s degree in Biology with an emphasis in Human Biology.  He then attended Creighton University School of Dentistry from 2017-2021 where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery Degree. Chad will be working at the Logan office practicing all aspects of comprehensive general dentistry and enjoys seeing and caring for the people of Cache Valley. Chad is a member of the American Dental Association, Utah Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry. 

Dr. Gunnell, his wife, Emilie, and their daughter Ellie, enjoyed living in Nebraska for 4 years, but are excited to be back “home” in Cache Valley with their family. Dr. Chad Gunnell enjoys making memories with his family, and being outside in the mountains that he missed while attending dental school. 

Accepting new patients of all ages.
