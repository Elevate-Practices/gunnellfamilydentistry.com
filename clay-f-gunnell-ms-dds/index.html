Dr. Clay F. Gunnell

MS, DDS

Dr. Clay Gunnell, is a Cache Valley native and a USU alumnus. He attended Creighton University for his dental training. He earned a Master of Science in oral biology with an emphasis in head and neck anatomy. His thesis was titled, “Anatomical Complications Related to Implant Dentistry.” Dr. Gunnell also earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Creighton University School of Dentistry. 

He loves being back in Cache Valley, enjoying the beautiful mountains and wonderful people here. Dr. Gunnell is thrilled to provide his patients with up-to-date general and specialized dental care, including extractions, root canals, dental implants, crown and bridges, preventative and aesthetic dentistry. He and his wife Megan, live in River Heights with their three children. He is a current member of the Utah Dental Association, American Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry.  He is currently accepting new patients of all ages.
