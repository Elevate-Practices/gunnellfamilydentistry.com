Specialty Applications in Logan, UT

Mouth guards, night guards and sleep apnea devices.

Protect your teeth from grinding (bruxism) and serious injury during sports with oral appliances designed specifically for your mouth. Below is a list of our specialty applications!

Our Dental Specialty Applications:

Mouth Guards

We are determined to make sure your mouth and teeth are well protected during sports activities, no matter what your passion is. We provide custom mouth guards to protect your teeth and mouth, allowing you to enjoy participating in your favorite sports.

Night Guards

Do you find yourself waking up with a sore jaw, gums and teeth? It can be beneficial to use a guard, or covering, to protect your teeth from grinding while sleeping. Similar to how a mouthguard protects teeth during sports, this layer prevents damage to the teeth that can occur naturally.

Sleep Apnea

Do you suffer from sleep apnea? We provide sleep apnea dentistry treatments designed to prevent teeth grinding. We create oral appliances to shift the jaw during sleep, helping to improve air flow and improve sleep.
